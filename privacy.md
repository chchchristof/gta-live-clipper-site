# Privacy Notice

**Effective date: November 19, 2026**  
**Project:** GTA Live Clipper  
**Contact:** [lastrideclips@gmail.com](mailto:lastrideclips@gmail.com)

This notice describes the GTA Live Clipper desktop app and this public information site. The app runs on the user's computer; the project does not operate a hosted user account or app database.

## 1. This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process ordinary connection and security information when it serves the pages, under GitHub's own terms and privacy practices. If you email the contact address, the email provider will process your message.

## 2. Information processed by the local app

Depending on your settings and use, the app may process livestream channel names and titles, public chat messages and usernames, stream audio and video, detected moments, transcripts, captions, finished clips, app settings, activity/status events, upload history, local paths, and OAuth credentials or tokens for accounts you choose to connect.

The helper runs locally and binds to the computer's loopback interface. Settings and tokens are stored in the app's local `.local` data folder; clips and related files are stored in app or user-selected output folders. The app applies owner-only file permissions to token files, but does not encrypt those files. Anyone who can access the relevant files or the user's computer account may be able to access them. Activity-log entries are stored in the browser's local storage.

## 3. Optional AI review

If AI review is enabled, the selected review provider may receive a transcript, detected-signal details, up to 50 recent public chat messages with usernames, and, if the still-frame option is enabled, up to three compressed still images sampled near a candidate moment. The app does not send full stream video, live audio, or account tokens in those review requests. If local Ollama is selected, review runs on the user's computer. If a cloud provider is selected, that provider processes the review information under its own terms and privacy notice.

## 4. TikTok and other connected services

Connecting TikTok is optional. The app may receive the account's Open ID and available profile information and store access/refresh tokens locally. When you request a post, or enable an automatic-posting feature, the selected video and caption are sent to TikTok using the permissions shown during authorization. TikTok controls its own processing and distribution. Disconnecting an account in the app removes its local token record, but may not revoke authorization at TikTok; use TikTok's account settings to revoke access as well.

Other optional connections may communicate with Twitch, Google/YouTube, Meta/Instagram, or an AI provider, depending on which features you enable. Each provider processes information under its own policies. The app does not send information to an optional provider unless you use or enable the feature that requires it.

## 5. Retention and deletion

Local settings, tokens, logs, clips, captions, and upload records generally remain on the computer until you remove them. You can remove a connected account from the app's Settings, revoke its authorization with the provider, and delete the app's local data and output files. Review and back up files before deleting them. Information already sent to a third-party provider is subject to that provider's retention and deletion controls.

## 6. Children and other people in stream content

The app can process stream and chat material that includes information about people of different ages. It does not provide a hosted user-account service. If you have a concern about a minor's information in local files or information sent to a connected provider, contact us and use the relevant provider's privacy controls. The project cannot access or delete files stored only on a user's computer or copies held by another provider.

## 7. Changes and contact

This notice will be updated if the app's data practices materially change. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to [lastrideclips@gmail.com](mailto:lastrideclips@gmail.com).
